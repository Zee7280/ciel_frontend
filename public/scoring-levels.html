How your Community Impact Score works

Every report you submit gets scored out of 100 — that score is your Community Impact Index (CII). It's what decides which badge and certificate lands in your name. Slide the score below and watch your badge change.

🎮 Score simulator — see your badge appear live

Drag the slider or type a number. Try 47, then 48, and watch what happens.

So what actually moves this number?

Your CII isn't just "did you finish the form." It's built from what you can actually back up — hours a supervisor verified, outcomes with real before-and-after numbers, evidence that holds up, and reflection that shows you actually thought about what you did. A short report with strong proof will beat a long report full of vague claims, every time.

⚖️ The boundary rule — no grey areas

Every score is checked from the top level down. Land exactly on a cutoff between two levels? You always get the higher one. No rounding against you, ever.

CII 58.0 → Level 3, not Level 2
CII 75.0 → Level 5, not Level 4
Badges and certificates are generated automatically once your report is reviewed and finalized. Check your dashboard any time to see your current CII and how far you are from your next badge.
